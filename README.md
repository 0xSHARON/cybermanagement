## 📖 What This Is

> *"You cannot govern what you cannot picture."*

The first half of this book builds the technical picture: how CPU privilege rings, operating systems, networking protocols, attacker kill chains, cryptography, and zero-trust identity architectures operate. The second half teaches the management craft: deciding what matters, quantitative risk modeling, developing security programmes, incident response, European regulations (NIS2, DORA, GDPR), and board-level fiduciary oversight.

- **100% Free & Open Resources**: Every video lecture, RFC standard, lab (OverTheWire, TryHackMe, PortSwigger), and tool is free and verified.
- **CISM Exam Domain Aligned**: Structured to align with ISACA CISM management core requirements.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 & NIST CSF 2.0**: Grounded in current global security frameworks.
